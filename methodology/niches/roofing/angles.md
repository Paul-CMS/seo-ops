# Roofing
Aliases: roofing-contractor, roof-repair, roof-replacement, roofers
Clients: Apex, Red Rover. Primary GBP category: Roofing contractor.

The niche module for the place prompt (Prompt B, `places.py prompt`). Placeholders: {PLACE} {STATE} {COUNTY} {NICHE}
{YEAR}. Full depth uses the angles 1-5 below (plus the fixed 6-8: places and entities, identity, local questions). Lite
depth uses the three FAQ-category angles. What a roof faces here is wind, water, sun and salt; what a roofer faces here
is the permit desk, the HOA and the insurer.

## Full-depth angles

### 1. Permits and approvals for {NICHE} work in {PLACE} ({YEAR})
- Which office issues permits for roofing work (town, city or county), with its official page.
- Which roofing jobs need a permit and which don't (repair vs replacement thresholds, any percentage rules), required
  inspections, and typical fees if officially published.
- Contractor licensing: state licence type required for roofing work, and any local business licence {PLACE} requires.
- Building code edition in force and any local amendments that affect roofing (wind, impact, fire, energy, flood).

### 2. Community and design rules affecting {NICHE} work
- Historic districts or design-review boards in {PLACE} and what they require for roofing (materials, colours,
  approval steps).
- Named HOAs, POAs or ARBs inside {PLACE} with published rules about roofing. Name each community and its rule, with
  the source.
- Contractor access rules: gates, passes, decals, work-hour limits.

### 3. Environment and conditions that affect {NICHE} in {PLACE}
- Climate factors relevant to roofs: hurricane and wind exposure (design wind speed if published), salt air, humidity,
  heat, hail, storms, flooding.
- Named storms or weather events in the last 10 years that affected {PLACE} and are relevant to roofs.
- Flood zones and elevation rules (including any "substantial improvement" rule) that change roofing work.

### 4. Housing stock and property types
- Age and type of homes, common roof types and materials, oceanfront or waterfront exposure, historic homes, gated
  communities, second homes or vacation rentals.

### 5. Money and incentives relevant to {NICHE} in {PLACE}
- Local, county, state or utility rebates, tax credits or programmes that apply to roofing work here (impact-resistant
  or cool-roof incentives, mitigation grants).
- Insurance factors specific to this area that affect roofs (wind and hail deductibles, roof-age rules, state
  mitigation discounts), from official or insurer sources only.

## Lite angles

### 1. Local cost or decision
- What local rule or condition changes the roofing decision or its cost in {PLACE}: the permit threshold, the code
  edition's wind rating, an HOA or historic-district approval, a flood or wind-zone rule. Official sources only.

### 2. Logistics in this area
- How roofing work is arranged in {PLACE}: which office permits it and how, gate or access rules, the weather season
  that limits work, material delivery constraints (islands, narrow streets, HOA work hours).

### 3. Trust in this area
- How a homeowner in {PLACE} checks a roofer: the state licence lookup page, any local registration, who handles
  complaints, and the area's storm-chaser or unlicensed-contractor warnings from official sources.

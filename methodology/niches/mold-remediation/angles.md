# Mold remediation
Aliases: mold-removal, mold-testing, mold-inspection, mould-remediation, water-damage-restoration
Clients: Gettysburg. Primary GBP category: Mold remediation service.

The niche module for the place prompt (Prompt B, `places.py prompt`). Placeholders: {PLACE} {STATE} {COUNTY} {NICHE}
{YEAR}. Full depth uses the angles 1-5 below (plus the fixed 6-8: places and entities, identity, local questions). Lite
depth uses the three FAQ-category angles. Health-adjacent (CM-040): a health or safety claim needs an official source or
none is made. What a mold customer faces is water, a house that holds it, and an insurer and a buyer who both ask for
paperwork.

## Full-depth angles

### 1. Licensing, certification and standards for {NICHE} in {PLACE} ({YEAR})
- Whether {STATE} licenses or certifies mold assessors, inspectors or remediators (many states do not; some do), the
  board and its lookup page, and any county or local registration.
- Which standards are used for the work and clearance (EPA mold guidance, IICRC S520, any {STATE} department of health
  or environment guidance), with the issuing body and edition.
- Related rules that change a job in older homes: asbestos and lead-paint rules (EPA, state), waste handling, and
  who inspects.

### 2. Permits and community rules affecting {NICHE} work
- Whether remediation, demolition or rebuild after remediation needs a permit in {PLACE} (town, city or county office),
  with its official page, and any inspection after repairs.
- Historic districts or design-review boards in {PLACE} and what they require when walls, windows or masonry are
  opened or replaced.
- Named HOAs, condo associations or landlord rules inside {PLACE} that govern mold reports or work access.

### 3. Water, humidity and flooding in {PLACE}
- Climate and humidity factors that drive mold: rainfall, humid months, freeze-thaw, basement and crawl-space moisture,
  high water tables, radon-mitigation interplay.
- Flooding history in the last 10 years (named storms, creek or river floods) and FEMA flood zones that affect
  {PLACE}; local drainage or stormwater programmes.

### 4. Housing stock and property types
- Age and construction of homes: stone or brick foundations, basements, crawl spaces, slab homes, historic homes,
  rental housing and student or seasonal rentals, vacation properties, commercial and agricultural buildings.

### 5. Insurance, disclosure and money
- {STATE} rules on homeowners' insurance coverage for mold and water damage, and the insurance department's guidance.
- Seller-disclosure and landlord-tenant rules about mold in {STATE}, and who enforces them locally.
- Disaster aid, state or county assistance and local programmes that help pay for water-damage or mold work, from
  official sources only.

## Lite angles

### 1. Local cost or decision
- What local rule or condition changes the mold-remediation decision or cost in {PLACE}: whether a licence or permit is
  required, a flood or high-water-table condition common here, an insurance or disclosure rule, an older-home
  condition (asbestos, lead) typical of local housing. Official sources only.

### 2. Logistics in this area
- How remediation is arranged in {PLACE}: the permit or health office to contact (if any), access to historic or
  restricted properties, the seasons when moisture problems spike, and the typical post-flood timeline.

### 3. Trust in this area
- How a homeowner in {PLACE} checks a mold company: the {STATE} licence or certification lookup (if one exists), the
  health department or consumer-protection office that takes complaints, and the standards a credible company follows.

# Stair lifts and mobility equipment
Aliases: stair-lift, stairlifts, mobility-equipment, mobility-equipment-supplier, home-mobility, accessibility-equipment
Clients: Home Mobile Medical (HMM). Primary GBP category: Mobility equipment supplier.

The niche module for the place prompt (Prompt B, `places.py prompt`). Placeholders: {PLACE} {STATE} {COUNTY} {NICHE}
{YEAR}. Full depth uses the angles 1-5 below (plus the fixed 6-8: places and entities, identity, local questions). Lite
depth uses the three FAQ-category angles. Health-adjacent (CM-040): a coverage, funding or safety claim needs an
official source or none is made. What a customer here faces is the staircase, the funding question and the install day.

## Full-depth angles

### 1. Codes, permits and licensing for lift installation in {PLACE} ({YEAR})
- Whether installing a stair lift, porch (vertical platform) lift or wheelchair lift needs a building, electrical or
  other permit in {PLACE} (town, city or county office), with its official page; which jobs are exempt.
- Contractor licensing: whether {STATE} licenses lift or accessibility-equipment installers (a limited-energy,
  elevator/lift or general-contractor licence), the board and its lookup page, and any local business licence.
- Code and standard editions in force that touch residential lifts and accessibility (ASME A18.1, the ICC A117.1 and
  residential code editions {STATE} has adopted) and any local amendments.

### 2. Homes, stairs and sites in {PLACE}
- Housing stock where stair lifts and platform lifts are fitted: older two- and three-storey homes, historic homes,
  raised or stilt homes with long exterior stairs, condos and townhomes, manufactured homes, retirement communities.
- Named condo associations, HOAs or historic-district boards in {PLACE} with published rules on exterior lifts or
  alterations. Name each community and its rule, with the source.
- Site conditions for exterior equipment: salt air, humidity, storm and flood exposure, flood-zone elevation rules that
  affect an outdoor lift.

### 3. Paying for it: programmes and funding that apply in {PLACE}
- Medicare, Medicaid and {STATE} waiver programmes: what each covers or does not cover for stair lifts, platform lifts
  and related equipment, from the programme's own pages.
- Veterans' benefits (VA home improvement and structural alterations grants) and how a veteran in {PLACE} applies.
- The {COUNTY} County or regional Area Agency on Aging, local home-modification or accessibility programmes, tax
  treatment from IRS or state sources, and any utility or charity programmes, with the contact page.

### 4. Care ecosystem and who sends the customer
- Hospitals, rehabilitation centres, home-health agencies and occupational-therapy services in and around {PLACE}
  that recommend or assess home modifications, with their official pages.
- Senior-housing, aging-in-place and caregiver resources run by the county, the state or {PLACE} (not other equipment
  suppliers).

### 5. Power, weather and service in {PLACE}
- Outage, hurricane or storm patterns relevant to battery-backed lifts and to emergency service, and any
  evacuation or special-needs registry the county runs.
- Geography that changes installation or service visits: islands, bridges, ferries, long rural roads (official
  transport or county sources).

## Lite angles

### 1. Local cost or decision
- What local rule, programme or condition changes the decision or the cost of a stair or platform lift in {PLACE}: a
  permit requirement, an HOA or historic-district approval, an area funding programme, an exterior-stairs situation
  common to local homes. Official sources only.

### 2. Logistics in this area
- How a lift installation is arranged in {PLACE}: the permit office (if any permit is required), access to older or
  gated communities, travel constraints (bridges, islands), and the local weather season that affects exterior
  installs.

### 3. Trust in this area
- How a household in {PLACE} checks an installer: the {STATE} licence lookup (if the state licenses this work), the
  local Area Agency on Aging or veterans' office that vets providers, and official consumer-protection warnings.

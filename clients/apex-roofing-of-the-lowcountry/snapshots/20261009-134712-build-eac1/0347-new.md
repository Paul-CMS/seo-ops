## What Makes Roofing in Bluffton Different?

Bluffton roofs face storm season and a split between very old and very new houses. The town sits about 20 feet above sea level, with the May River through its center and the New River on its southwest edge, so its homes are low and near the water when hurricanes threaten in summer and early fall. Wind-driven rain in that season is the test a Bluffton roof has to pass. In Old Town, a National Historic District since 1996, some homes date to the 1800s. Most of the rest of Bluffton is new: the town grew from 1,275 people in 2000 to 27,716 in 2020.

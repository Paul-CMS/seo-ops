Key points for the Ridgeland and Okatie briefs:

Ridgeland
- Incorporated town in Jasper County (incorporated 1894, county seat). A Ridgeland postal address does not prove the property is inside town limits. Ridgeland runs its own planning and permit process.
- Named residential areas: Bees Creek Plantation; The Groves at Bees Creek (new-home neighborhood off Bees Creek Road); a downtown residential core (an area, not a subdivision). Use "Ridgeland and nearby residential communities" if the boundary is unverified.
- Do not say Ridgeland has a higher roof-wear rate than coastal towns. Do not repeat a "130 mph minimum" as a Ridgeland code requirement (one competitor claim, unverified).

Okatie
- Unincorporated community that spans Beaufort AND Jasper counties. Do not write that every Okatie permit goes through Beaufort County (one competitor page says so; it is too broad).
- Named communities: Oldfield (gated, Okatie River, marsh); Callawassie Island (its own published roof standards and architectural review); Spring Island (its own rules; do not copy Callawassie rules to it).
- Callawassie roof standards (published ARC guidelines): shingles need a 40-year or "Lifetime" warranty; cedar, slate and metal are acceptable; vent flashing and vents painted to match; raw aluminium or galvanised flashing not allowed; exterior changes after construction need architectural-review approval. These are community rules, not guarantees of roof life.
- Permit and community architectural approval are separate processes. Keep them separate on the page.

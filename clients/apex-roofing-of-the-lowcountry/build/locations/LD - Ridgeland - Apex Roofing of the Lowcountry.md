## Was Ridgeland Really Built on a Ridge?

Yes. Ridgeland's town center sits on a low ridge, and the name points to some of the highest land in Jasper County. The town was incorporated in 1894 and is the county seat, so the Jasper County Courthouse and the County's planning and building office are both here. The 2020 census counted about 3,750 people and 1,151 housing units, and only 9.6% of those homes stood vacant. For a roofer, that means a small town of houses that are mostly lived in, each with its own roof history.

### Under Ridgeland's architectural standards, which roofing materials can I choose?

Asphalt or fiberglass shingles must be architectural grade and sent to the Town for approval, under the architectural standards in Ridgeland's zoning ordinance. Metal is on the list too: galvanized steel in corrugated, 5V crimp or standing seam panels, plus galvalume and copper. So are wood shingles. The rules cover all structures in the Town's T2.5 to T5 zones.

Flashing must be copper, lead or anodized aluminum, and skylights must sit flat, out of sight from the street. The Town's architectural review checklist, last updated in 2021, prints these rules. Ask the Town's planning office for the current wording and your lot's zone. Apex installs shingle and metal roofs, so your choice can start there.

### Am I inside Ridgeland's town limits, and does that change who issues my roof permit?

Yes, it changes who you ask. Outside the town limits, Jasper County issues the permit, and it lists roof replacement among the jobs that need one. Inside the limits, the Town of Ridgeland runs its own permit process, with its own Building Official. A Ridgeland mailing address does not prove which side you are on.

Both offices are in town. Jasper County Planning and Building is at 358 Third Avenue, Room 202, (843) 717-3650. Town Hall is at 1 Town Square, (843) 726-7516. Ask the Town if your address is inside the limits, and settle the permit before the work date.

## Ever Visited the Historic Sites in and Around Ridgeland?

As the county seat, Ridgeland keeps landmarks from Jasper County's courthouse life and its Revolutionary and Civil War past.

- **Old Baptist Church** — once used as a headquarters by Union forces.
- **Church of the Holy Trinity** — listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1982.

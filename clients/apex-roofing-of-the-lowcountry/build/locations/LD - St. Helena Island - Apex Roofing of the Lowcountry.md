## Why Does St. Helena Island Still Feel So Rural?

Beaufort County calls St. Helena Island "one of Beaufort County's remaining rural spaces." Its code describes forests, family farms and Gullah/Geechee family compounds, linked by two-lane roads under a canopy of trees. Wide marshes surround the island, most of all on its southeastern side. US 21, the Sea Island Parkway, crosses the island and carries traffic on toward Harbor Island and the islands past it. On the marsh edges, wind and weather reach a roof straight off the open water.

### Must a new roof on St. Helena Island follow the Cultural Protection Overlay?

No. Beaufort County's Cultural Protection Overlay sets no rules for roofs or roofing materials. It limits large-scale growth on St. Helena Island instead. Where the overlay applies, Section 3.4.50 of the County's Community Development Code keeps land uses to the base zoning. It also bans gated communities, resorts and golf courses.

The County says the overlay exists to keep the island's rural character and protect its cultural, natural and historic resources. Its text names the island as home to one of the largest Gullah/Geechee communities on the southeast coast. Re-roofing an existing house is not one of the uses it bans. Before work starts, ask Beaufort County what your parcel needs, since other County rules may still apply.

### Has Apex worked on Harbor Island, past St. Helena on US 21?

Yes. Apex Roofing of the Lowcountry roofs homes on Harbor Island, and the only way to drive there is across St. Helena Island. US 21 is the one road onto Harbor Island and the islands near it. Harbor Island is a gated home community, so anyone coming to work on your roof has to get through the gate.

Before the job, ask the Harbor Island Owners Association what it needs from you to let a contractor in. Ask, too, whether it has rules on roof color or material. Bring those answers to your free estimate so the roof plan starts from them.

## How Can You Spend a Day on St. Helena Island?

Penn Center is the natural place to start a day on St. Helena Island. Beaufort County notes that the Penn Center area is a National Historic Landmark and holds two of the four sites of Reconstruction Era National Historical Park.

- **York W. Bailey Museum and the Penn Center campus** — the museum at 16 Penn Center Circle West, with docent-led or self-guided tours of the campus, a nature trail and an overlook.
- **Penn Center Heritage Days** — the annual Heritage Days Celebration, held each November.

## What Historic Places Mark St. Helena Island?

St. Helena Island's historic places include an 1898 coastal battery and two sites on the National Register of Historic Places.

- **Fort Fremont** — a battery at Lands End, built in 1898 to guard the entrance to the Beaufort River, now a preserve owned by Beaufort County.
- **Indian Hill Site** — listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
- **Knights of Wise Men Lodge** — listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

## What Should You Know About Roofing in a Low-Lying Town Like Port Royal?

Port Royal's listed elevation is 3 feet, so much of the town is barely above the sea when a storm comes in. The 1893 Sea Islands Hurricane destroyed much of the phosphate industry here and held back the town's growth. Growth came back much later. The population went from 3,950 in 2000 to 10,678 in 2010 and 14,220 in 2020, a large share of it from annexing Parris Island and its on-base housing. For a roof, low ground and storm season are the same on an older Old Village home as on a newer house in an annexed area.

### Does a big roof job on a Port Royal home trigger the flood-zone 50% rule?

It can, if the roof is part of work that adds up to half the building's value. The Town of Port Royal's flood guide sets the rule. When additions, remodeling or repairs reach 50% of a building's market value, the whole building must be raised above the base flood elevation, the level a big flood is expected to reach.

Under chapter 9 of the Town's code, permits count together for five years. So a new roof plus other permitted work on the same house in that time can add up toward the 50% line. A job done in phases is totaled from the first permit, and repairs after damage are added up too. In a flood zone, a building permit comes before any construction. Ask the Town's building office whether your address is in a flood zone and how it will count your job. Once you know, Apex can plan the roof work to fit.

### Who should repair the roof on an older house in Port Royal's Old Village?

Choose a roofer who shows you the damage and repairs it in kind. Under the Town of Port Royal's published historic overlay rules, repairs, replacements and routine maintenance that are not an alteration need no Certificate of Appropriateness. A change to a home on the Town Historic Register that can be seen from the street may need one.

Not every Old Village house is on the Register, so ask Town Planning at 843-986-2207 about your address. The same rules name a deteriorating roof as a sign of neglect on a Register property, so a leak is worth fixing soon. At Apex, Ralph and Pierce Brehany both get on the roof. Repairs use corrosion-resistant flashing and synthetic underlayment, and you get photos showing the work. Customers mention crews who show up promptly each day.

## Which Historic Sites Can You Visit in Port Royal?

Port Royal's historic sites include a British fort, a Civil War camp and two National Register buildings.

- **Fort Frederick Heritage Preserve** — a county-managed park with the tabby ruins of a British-built fort.
- **Camp Saxton Site** — where the Emancipation Proclamation was read on January 1, 1863, now part of the Reconstruction Era National Historical Park.
- **Union Church of Port Royal** — listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
- **F.W. Scheper Store** — listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

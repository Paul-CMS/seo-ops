## Why Are So Many Lady's Island Homes in Newer Subdivisions?

Lady's Island is mostly residential. Since the late 20th century, land that was once plantation and timber tracts has been turned into residential subdivisions, so much of the island's housing is fairly recent. The island sits about 16 feet above sea level. Its land mixes maritime upland forest with tidal and freshwater wetlands. A typical roof job here is on a newer house set among woods and marsh, not on a historic one.

## What Should You Ask a Roofing Contractor About a Lady's Island Re-Roof?

Ask who covers your address and how the flood rule counts roof work. On nearby Dataw Island, ask if Apex works there.

### Does my Lady's Island address fall under the City of Beaufort or the County?

Most likely the County. Only small portions of Lady's Island are inside the City of Beaufort, and most of the island is unincorporated Beaufort County. This matters for a re-roof. The City lists a reroof among the projects that still need its Building Code review. A home outside the City goes to Beaufort County Building Codes instead.

To check your address, call the City's Community Development office at 843-525-7011, or look the parcel up in the County's property records. Bring the answer to your free estimate, so the job is planned around the right office's review.

### Could a new roof on a Lady's Island home set off Beaufort County's 50% flood rule?

Only if the work costs half of your home's market value or more. For homes in unincorporated Lady's Island, Beaufort County's Flood Damage Prevention Ordinance applies. It calls any repair, rehabilitation, addition or other improvement that reaches 50 percent of the building's market value, measured before work starts, a "substantial improvement."

The County sets market value from the assessor's building value or from an appraisal. Two kinds of work are left out: fixing code violations the County has already found, and changes to a historic structure that keep its historic status. The ordinance also adds up work done over time, so ask the County how it will count your projects. You can request an Elevation Certificate for your property on the County's Flood Information page. If your home is inside the City of Beaufort, ask the City instead.

### Will Apex re-roof our home on Dataw Island, near Lady's Island?

Yes. Apex Roofing of the Lowcountry re-roofs homes on Dataw Island, a private island community near Lady's Island. Dataw's published Design Guidelines (March 2025) treat re-roofing as a minor project that needs an application to the island's Architectural Review Board. Putting back the same color and material takes only a signed application.

A new color or material must be named, with samples. The Guidelines list slate tile, standing seam metal and asphalt shingles. They require mildew-resistant shingles on any new or replaced roof, and a non-glare finish on metal. Shingle samples come from the approved list at the review board's office. Apex installs both CertainTeed and GAF shingles as a factory-certified installer, so you can start from either brand and check it against that list. The crew also cleans up each day and runs a magnet over the yard for nails. One homeowner wrote in 2026 that the clean-up was superb each day.

## Where Can You Get Outdoors on Lady's Island?

Lady's Island's outdoor spots include a park on the marsh, a lake trail and a county boat ramp.

- **Whitehall Park** — 9.72 acres of pine and live oak on a salt marsh point on the Beaufort River, at 120 White Hall Drive.
- **Crystal Lake Park** — a 25-acre county park at 124 Lady's Island Drive, with a half-mile loop trail around the lake, half boardwalk and half earthen path.
- **White Hall Boat Landing** — a county landing with a four-lane ramp onto Lady's Island–Factory Creek, and parking for 16.

## Which Landmarks Stand Out on Lady's Island?

Lady's Island's landmarks include a swing bridge, an airport and a site where the Coosaw people once lived.

- **Richard V. Woods Memorial Bridge** — the swing bridge to downtown Beaufort, opened on December 17, 1959, and listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2023.
- **Beaufort Executive Airport** — a county-run general aviation airport on about 110 acres, also called Beaufort County Airport.
- **Coosaw Point** — the island's northeastern point, where the Coosaw people lived on the high ground.

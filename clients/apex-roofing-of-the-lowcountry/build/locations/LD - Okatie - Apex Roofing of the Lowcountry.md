### Do I need ARC approval to replace my roof on Callawassie Island?

Yes. Callawassie's published guidelines, updated December 12, 2023, say any exterior change made without ARC approval is a violation. A new roof is an exterior change, so get approval first. The same guidelines set which materials and details the ARC accepts.

- Asphalt or fiberglass shingles with a 40-year or approved "Lifetime" warranty, plus cedar shakes or shingles, slate and metal.
- A roof pitch of at least 6/12, meaning the roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches across.
- Vent pipes, vent flashing, roof vents and fans painted to match the roof.
- No raw aluminum or galvanized flashing; flashing other than copper painted to blend in.

The 40-year figure is the shingle warranty the guidelines ask for, not a promise of how long a roof lasts. A county building permit is a separate step.

### Is my Okatie roofing permit handled by Beaufort County or Jasper County?

It depends on which county your home sits in, because Okatie lies in both Beaufort and Jasper counties. Jasper County's Planning and Building department says a building permit is required for work that includes roof replacement. For a home in unincorporated Beaufort County, the office to contact is Beaufort County Building Codes.

Check which county your parcel is in before you call either one.

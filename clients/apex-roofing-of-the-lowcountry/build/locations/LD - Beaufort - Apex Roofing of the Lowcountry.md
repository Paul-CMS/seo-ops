## What Makes Beaufort a Different Place to Roof?

Beaufort's roofs sit on some of the oldest houses in the state. Chartered in 1711, Beaufort is the second-oldest city in South Carolina, and it sits amid a marshy estuary, mostly on Port Royal Island. The 1893 hurricane and a 1907 fire caused extensive destruction in the city. Today, 304 acres of Beaufort form a National Historic Landmark district known for its antebellum architecture, while Pigeon Point is mostly one-story homes from the early 20th century. A roof job in Beaufort can mean either kind of house, and each brings its own questions.

## What Do Owners of Historic Beaufort Homes Ask a Roofer?

Owners of older Beaufort homes ask which City review a roof needs, whether metal is allowed and whether an old roof can be saved.

### Does a roof replacement in Beaufort's Historic District need review board approval?

It depends on the house and the work. A new roof material on a contributing building in the Historic District goes to the City's Historic Review Board. The City's current form lists an in-kind reroof, with the same material put back, as staff-level review instead. Staff can still send any of those to the Board.

City code calls for a Certificate of Appropriateness for construction work in any historic district. The City's historic sites survey lists show whether your house is contributing. A reroof also goes through the City's building code review, which is a separate step. Apex coordinates the roofing-material approval with the City's historic review, so the review is part of the plan before a date is set.

### Can a historic Beaufort home get a metal roof?

Often it can, but the type of metal roof matters. The City's Metal Roofing Guidelines say mechanical-lock standing seam may be right for a contributing house that is replacing a non-historic roof, such as asphalt shingle or 5V metal. Snap-lock standing seam is not, because its seams cannot be folded by hand at the hips and ridges.

On a contributing house, the change of material goes to the Historic Review Board. On a non-contributing house, City staff review it case by case. Apex installs standing seam metal roofs [LINK → /metal-roofing/ · reader wants Apex's metal-roofing service page]. Ask at your free estimate which seam type your house needs under the Guidelines.

### Can the old terne metal roof on my Beaufort house be saved?

It may be, and the City of Beaufort would rather keep it. The City's staff policy is to preserve as many historic terne metal roofs as possible. Before one comes off, staff ask for photos to judge the damage. If the roof can be kept, they recommend recoating it. If not, they recommend a suitable metal roof, not asphalt shingles.

Terne needs regular recoating, and neglected spots rust until the roof stops keeping water out. So you need a straight answer about your roof's condition.

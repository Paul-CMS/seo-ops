## How Close to the Ocean Do Fripp Island Roofs Sit?

Very close. Fripp Island is a barrier island with a listed elevation of 0 feet, and a wide system of marsh and estuary separates it from St. Helena Island. The island is a private, gated vacation resort of homes and condominiums, run by the Fripp Island Property Owners Association, and nearly one-third of its lots are still undeveloped. It had 963 residents at the 2020 census, but in summer the population can rise to about 5,000. On a low island this open to the ocean, wind exposure is the first thing a roof plan has to account for.

## How Do Fripp Island Owners Plan a Roof Job With a Residential Roofer?

Three things shape a Fripp Island roof job: the island's review board, the security gate, and how you follow the work when you are not there.

### Would a same-color re-roof on Fripp Island still need an ARB application?

Yes. A re-roof in the same color and material needs no approval from the Fripp Island Architectural Review Board. Even so, the property owners' association's homeowners manual says you must still complete an application and post a permit, and there is no cost. The ARB reviews single-family homes, while condominiums are handled by their own regimes.

The ARB meets on the first and third Thursday of each month at 8:30 AM. Applications are due at its office by 4:30 PM on the Friday before. Thinking about a new color or a different material? Ask the ARB first, because its roofing standards sit behind the homeowners' login.

### Why does a roofing crew need clearance at the Fripp Island security gate?

Fripp Island is private, so Security officers from the property owners' association run its gate day and night. The association charges access fees to some commercial vendors and service providers. A contractor whose vehicle carries a Fripp Island Contractor decal pays no access fee. Weekend work needs Security's permission, and it must be quiet work.

A contractor who arrives without the fee will not be turned away if Security can reach you and you agree to pay within 14 days. Security then emails you an IOU, so keep your phone close on work days. Ask how the gate will be handled when you book your estimate, so nothing holds up the first day.

### If I'm away from Fripp Island, how will I know how my roof job is going?

You will see it in photos. Apex Roofing of the Lowcountry sends you photos of the work, and a longer job brings new progress photos every day. Ralph and Pierce Brehany, the father and son behind Apex, climb onto the roof themselves. So the pictures come from the people doing the work.

That matters on an island this open to the Atlantic. FEMA's coastal construction guidance says the highest uplift forces on a roof occur at its corners, edges and ridge lines. Those spots are hard to judge from the ground. One owner wrote in 2020 that, unable to check on the job from Florida, they got emails and photos from Ralph before and after.

## What Keeps People Busy on Fripp Island?

Fripp Island has two golf courses and a marina.

- **Ocean Point Golf Links** — built within ten years of the bridge's 1964 opening.
- **Ocean Creek Golf Course** — designed by Davis Love III and built in 1994.
- **The marina** — expanded during the same 1994 building phase.

## What Sights Are on and Around Fripp Island?

Fripp Island's sights include its bridge, its wildlife and a state park next door.

- **Fripp Inlet Bridge** — the 2,067-foot main bridge onto the island, completed in 1964 and now toll-free.
- **The all-faiths chapel** — an interfaith chapel on the island.
- **The Audubon bird preserve and loggerhead turtle hatchery** — the whole island is an Audubon-designated bird preserve, and it is a major loggerhead turtle hatchery.
- **Hunting Island State Park** — just north of Fripp, with five miles of beach and a lighthouse first built in 1859.

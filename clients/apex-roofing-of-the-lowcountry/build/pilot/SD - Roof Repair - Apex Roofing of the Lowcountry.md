## How Do Our Roofers Fix a Damaged Roof in Beaufort and Jasper Counties?

Our roofers fix a damaged roof by finding where the water gets in, not just where the stain shows, and then repairing that spot with the right flashing and matching materials. Apex is a father-and-son company, and Ralph and Pierce both get on the roof.

A repair visit goes in this order:

1. **Free estimate.** You tell us what you see. There is no minimum service-call charge and no travel fee inside our service area.
2. **On the roof.** We go up and trace the leak to its source. Water often runs along the deck before it drips, so the ceiling stain is rarely the spot to patch.
3. **Find the cause.** We check the flashing, the valleys, the shingles and the wood under them.
4. **Repair.** We fix the cause, replace any rotted wood, and seal the area so it sheds water again.
5. **Clean up.** We clean up each day and run a magnet over the ground for loose nails.
6. **Show you the work.** We send you photos of the work, with progress photos each day on a longer job.

Flashing comes first. Flashing is the metal that seals the joints where the roof meets a chimney, a wall, a vent pipe or a valley. In the words of the owners, a lot of water damage can be stopped "by using enough flashing and not just trying to use a tube of silicone to make it go faster and cheaper." Sealant alone cracks. Proper flashing lasts.

We match the repair to the roof you have. Apex is a certified factory installer of both CertainTeed and GAF shingles, so a repair can use the same brand already on your home. On repairs we use corrosion-resistant flashing, which stands up to salt air, and synthetic underlayment under the new shingles.

Lowcountry weather is hard on roofs. Salt air wears down metal flashing. Wind lifts the tabs on older three-tab shingles and loosens ridge caps. Algae and moss hold water against the shingles, and a poorly vented attic can rot the deck from below.

How do you know a roof needs repair? Look for a stain after heavy rain. Look for lifted or missing shingles, or grit in the gutters. In the attic, look for damp, dark wood.

## Which Roof Repairs Do We Handle?

Apex fixes the parts of a home's roof that let water in. That means shingles, flashing, ridge caps, rotted wood at the edges and deck, and hidden leaks.

### Shingle Repair and Replacement

Damaged shingles are replaced one by one with a matching CertainTeed or GAF shingle. Lifted tabs are reset and sealed so wind cannot get under them again.

### Flashing Replacement at Chimneys, Walls and Valleys

Loose or corroded flashing is removed and replaced. This covers chimney flashing, step flashing along walls, valley flashing and pipe boots.

### Ridge Cap and Starter Strip Repair

The ridge cap runs along the peak, and the starter strip runs along the bottom edge. When either one lifts or splits, wind-driven rain gets in. We replace the loose pieces and fasten them down.

### Soffit, Fascia and Roof Deck Repair

Rotted plywood decking, dry-rotted soffits and soft fascia boards are cut out and replaced with new wood before the roof is closed up. Covering rotted wood with new shingles only hides the problem.

### Roof Leak Repair

A leak that shows up in one or two places usually comes from one failed spot: a flashing joint, a cracked shingle or an open seam. We trace the water back to that spot and repair it there.

### What is the 25% rule for roofing?

The 25% rule is a code rule in some states. If more than a quarter of a roof section is fixed or redone in a set time, the whole section must meet current code. Your local building department decides if a rule like this applies to your home.

### How much does a roof repair cost?

The cost of a roof repair depends on the size and kind of damage and what turns up under the shingles, like rotted decking. Roof pitch, access and matching the existing shingle also change the price. Apex gives a free estimate with no minimum service-call charge.

### Should I repair or replace my roof?

Repair is the right call when the damage is limited to one area and the layers underneath are still sound. Replacement makes sense when the underlayment, valleys or deck have failed across the roof. The difference is what you cannot see from the ground, so we look under the shingles before we give you an answer. A replacement roof carries a manufacturer warranty. After one recent GAF roof replacement, the homeowner wrote in a Google review that Ralph handed over the GAF System Plus Limited Warranties when the job was done.

### Can a roof be repaired in a day?

Often, yes: a small repair can be quick, but one that uncovers rotted decking takes longer, because the wood must be replaced first. In one Google review, a homeowner with a slow leak in a seven-year-old metal roof says Pierce found it and fixed it in under 15 minutes. Your estimate will tell you how long your repair should take.

### What should I do if I have an emergency roof leak?

If water is coming through the ceiling right now, catch it and move what you can. Then call a roofer to stop the leak. An active leak or fresh storm damage is an emergency, not a scheduled repair.

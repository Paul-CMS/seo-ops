## How Does a Roofer Lay a Slate Roof in Beaufort and Jasper Counties?

A roofer lays a slate roof by checking that the house can carry the stone first, then strengthening the framing if it needs it, and getting any historic-district approval before a single tile goes on. Here is how that works with Apex.

1. **The framing check.** Slate is heavy. It weighs roughly 800–1,500 lbs per square against 200–350 for architectural shingles. A square is 100 square feet of roof. So before anything else, we look at the rafters and roof framing to see whether they can carry that load.
2. **Reinforcement, if needed.** When the framing falls short, it gets stronger before the slate goes up. That can mean sister rafters, where a new board is fastened alongside an old rafter, or purlins, which are boards run across the rafters to spread the weight.
3. **Approval in historic districts.** Homes in the City of Beaufort's historic districts may need the roof material approved first. Apex coordinates that approval with the City of Beaufort Board of Architectural Review.
4. **The work on the roof.** Ralph Brehany and his son Pierce own Apex, and both of them get up on the roof. [NEED: slate install steps — underlayment, fastening, flashing, if they are to be described]
5. **Photos and clean-up.** You get photos of the work, plus progress photos each day on a longer job. The site is cleaned each day, and a magnet goes over the yard to pick up loose nails.

Slate is not a do-it-yourself roof. The tiles crack if you step on them the wrong way, and a cracked tile lets in water. Walking, cutting and fastening slate takes a roofer who has done it before.

## Natural, Synthetic or a Repair: Which Slate Work Do You Need?

Apex does three kinds of slate work: new roofs in genuine Vermont slate, new roofs in DaVinci Roofscapes synthetic slate, and repairs to slate roofs that are mostly sound.

### Genuine Vermont Slate

Genuine Vermont slate is quarried stone, and Apex installs it in standard grades suited to coastal wind requirements. Natural slate comes in several colors, and the color is in the stone itself, not painted on. When you choose tiles, you are choosing a color, a size and a thickness.

### DaVinci Roofscapes Synthetic Slate

DaVinci Roofscapes synthetic slate is made to look like natural slate without being stone. Its Province Slate tile is a composite made from virgin resins, according to the maker. Because it is not stone, it is a lighter option for a home that wants the slate look. DaVinci Roofscapes is the synthetic slate brand Apex installs.

### Slate Roof Repair

A slate roof repair from Apex means replacing the broken tiles one at a time when most of the roof is still sound. We also re-flash the penetrations. Those are the places where a chimney, vent pipe or skylight comes through the roof, and the metal flashing around them is a common source of leaks. On an older or historic home, a repair can keep the original slate roof in place.

## What Should Lowcountry Owners Know Before Choosing Slate?

### How much does a slate roof cost?

The price of a slate roof turns mostly on the size of the roof, then on natural or synthetic slate, the pitch, any framing reinforcement, removing the old roof and historic-district approval. A repair depends on how many tiles and how much flashing need work. The free estimate puts a number on yours.

### What is the biggest disadvantage of a slate roof?

The biggest disadvantage of a slate roof is its weight: natural slate is far heavier than architectural shingles, so not every house can carry it without extra framing. It also costs more up front, and a broken tile needs a roofer who knows slate.

### Can a slate roof be repaired instead of replaced?

Yes, a slate roof can be repaired instead of replaced when most of the tiles are still sound: the bad tiles are swapped out one by one and leaking flashing is renewed. Watch for cracked, slipped or missing tiles, hail damage, and leaks around a chimney or vent.

## Which Roofing Materials Can Go on Your New Roof?

A new roof from Apex can be architectural asphalt shingles, metal, slate or synthetic roofing. The right one depends on your budget, your home and how long you plan to stay.

### Architectural Asphalt Shingles

Architectural shingles are thick asphalt shingles made in layers. They are the most common pick for a new roof. Apex installs both CertainTeed and GAF architectural shingles.

### Metal Roofing

A metal roof is a step up in cost from shingles. It stands up well to salt air and strong wind, which matters near the coast.

### Slate Roofing

Slate is a natural stone roof and the heaviest choice. The roof framing is checked first to make sure it can carry the weight.

### Synthetic Roofing

Synthetic roofing is an engineered product made to look like natural slate. It weighs much less than real stone, so it suits homes that cannot carry a slate roof.

### Storm-Damage Roof Replacement

Sometimes a storm damages a roof too badly to patch. Once the home is safe and dry, a full replacement follows the same steps as any other new roof, from tear-off to final clean-up.

In 2026, a typical whole-home roof replacement in the Lowcountry runs about $14,000 to $20,000 with architectural asphalt shingles. - how many old layers must be torn off

## How Does a Roofing Contractor Put a Metal Roof on a Home in the South Carolina Lowcountry?

A roofing contractor puts a metal roof on a home in three stages: a talk about what you need, approval from your community, and an install built to keep out wind and salt air. Here is how it goes with Apex.

1. **Free consultation.** We start by talking through your home, your budget and how long you plan to stay. Customers often mention that the owners take the time to explain the products.
2. **Color submittals and ARB submissions.** Many Lowcountry communities have an architectural review board, or ARB, that must approve a new roof color. If yours does, Apex prepares the color submittals and the ARB paperwork.
3. **The install.** The panels go on with concealed fasteners. That means the screws sit under the raised seams, out of the weather. The whole system is built for wind-uplift resistance, which is how well a roof holds its panels down when hurricane winds try to pull them up. 4. **Wrap-up.** You get daily photos of the panels going on, and the yard gets a magnet sweep for nails before we leave.

The coast is the reason for each of those choices. Salt air eats at bare metal and open screw heads. Summer sun fades a weak paint finish. Humid air keeps everything damp. Hidden fasteners and coated panels are how a metal roof holds up here.

## Which Standing Seam Metal Roof Options Can You Choose From?

With a standing seam metal roof from Apex, you choose the panel coating, the panel profile and the color. If you already have a metal roof, the choice may be repair or replacement.

### Kynar 500 and Hylar 5000 Coated Panels

Apex installs standing seam panels coated with Kynar 500 or Hylar 5000. Both are PVDF finishes, a type of factory-applied paint resin made to hold its color in strong sun and resist chalking. Near the water, the coating also shields the metal underneath from salt air. The coated panels Apex installs carry a 40-year warranty against chalking and fading.

### Panel Profiles, Colors and HOA Color Submittals

Standing seam panels come in a range of profiles and colors. The profile is the shape of the panel and the look of its raised seams. If you live in an HOA community, settle on a color early. The board often has to sign off before work can start, and Apex puts the submittal together for you.

### Metal Roof Repair or Replacement

A damaged metal roof is not always a lost cause. Apex looks at the roof and weighs a repair against a full replacement before recommending either one. The answer depends on how much of the roof is damaged, what shape the panels and their finish are in, and whether water has reached the wood deck below.

## Which Metal Roof Questions Do Lowcountry Roofers Hear Most?

### How much does a metal roof cost?

In 2026, a standing seam metal roof on a typical Lowcountry home runs about $24,000 to $45,000 or more, and the price moves with the size of the roof. That is a regional range from published cost guides, not Apex's price. The main cost drivers are the size and pitch of the roof, the panel profile and coating, tearing off the old roof, and the vents and chimneys the panels must fit around. Your free estimate gives the real figure.

### Is a metal roof worth the extra cost over shingles?

Yes, if you plan to stay in the home and want a roof built for salt air and hurricane wind. Architectural shingles run about $14,000 to $20,000, so standing seam, priced above, costs more up front. The extra buys hidden screws, wind-uplift resistance and coated panels with a long finish warranty. If you may sell soon, shingles can fit better.

### What are the biggest problems with metal roofs?

Metal roofs have three common problems: leaks at open screws and at pipe and vent openings, paint that wears down in salt air, and rain noise. The concealed-fastener, coated panels Apex installs are built to deal with the first two.

Whether a metal roof can go over existing shingles depends on the building code, the panel maker's instructions and the condition of the roof underneath.

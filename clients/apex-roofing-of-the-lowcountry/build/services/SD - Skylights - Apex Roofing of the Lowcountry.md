## How Should a Residential Roofer Add or Fix a Skylight in the South Carolina Lowcountry?

A residential roofer should find out why a skylight is failing before fixing anything, and should frame a new opening the right way before adding a skylight. Apex does both, and you get the price in writing first. Here is how a skylight job goes.

1. **Find the cause.** When water comes in while it rains, the problem is usually the flashing or the curb. Flashing is the metal that seals the skylight to the roof. The curb is the raised frame the skylight sits on. When fog or drops show up between the panes and it has not rained, the sealed unit has failed. That is the glass and the sealed space between the panes.

## Which Skylight Job Fits Your Home: a New Unit, New Glass or New Flashing?

Apex does four kinds of skylight work. We add new skylights to roofs that are already on. We put new glass in fogged units. We fix flashing and curbs. And we replace a skylight that is past repair.

### New Skylight Installation in an Existing Roof

A new skylight brings daylight into a room from above. To add one, we cut an opening in the roof deck and frame it so the roof stays strong around the hole. This is the biggest skylight job, because it changes the structure of the roof.

### Sealed-Unit Replacement for Fogged Skylight Glass

Fog between the panes means the seal around the glass has failed and moist air has gotten in. Wiping the glass will not clear it. Replacing the sealed unit gives you clear glass again without replacing the whole skylight.

### Skylight Flashing and Curb Repair

A skylight that leaks in the rain can still have a sound unit with a failed seal to the roof. In that case, we repair the flashing and the curb around the skylight and leave the skylight in place.

### Full Skylight Replacement

Some skylights are past repair. When a unit can no longer be fixed, the whole skylight comes out and a new one goes in. ## Is a Skylight a Good Idea for Your Lowcountry Home?

### How much does it cost to install a skylight?

The cost to install a skylight depends most on whether a new opening must be cut and framed in the roof, or whether an old skylight just needs new glass or new flashing. The roof type and pitch, the size and type of skylight, and any community approval also change the price. Apex lists each part in a free, itemized written estimate.

### What are the downsides of a skylight?

The main downsides of a skylight are leaks where it meets the roof, fog between the panes when the glass seal fails, and extra heat and glare from the sun. A leak is fixed at the flashing or curb, and fogged glass is fixed with a new sealed unit.

### Are skylights worth it?

A skylight can be worth it when a room gets little or no daylight, such as a hallway, a bathroom or a room with no outside wall. [NEED: cited source on skylight energy performance — researcher] Before you decide, weigh the daylight you gain against the heat and glare a skylight can let in.

### What type of skylight works on a metal roof?

A skylight on a standing seam metal roof needs a unit and flashing made for metal. The flashing has to fit the raised seams so water cannot get in where the skylight meets the panels.

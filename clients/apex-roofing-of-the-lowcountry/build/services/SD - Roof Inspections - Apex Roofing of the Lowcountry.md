## What Happens When a Roofer Checks Your Roof in the South Carolina Lowcountry?

When a roofer from Apex checks your roof, one of the owners climbs up, looks at each part of it up close and then tells you plainly what shape it is in. Apex is run by Ralph and Pierce Brehany, a father and son. One of them does the climbing himself.

Most roof trouble starts in places you cannot see from the yard. The owners say the parts that decide how long a roof lasts are the hidden ones, and flashing most of all. So the check covers:

- **Shingles.** Missing, lifted, cracked or creased shingles, and loose grit washing into the gutters.
- **Flashing.** The metal strips that keep water out where the roof butts up against a chimney, a wall or a vent pipe. Salt air and heat can loosen it or eat it away.
- **Valleys.** The low lines where two slopes of the roof meet and water runs off.
- **Ridge cap.** The capping shingles over the top of the roof, which wind tends to pry up first.
- **The roof deck.** Soft or spongy spots underfoot can mean the wood below has rotted.
- **Signs of a cheap patch.** A bead of caulk where there should be metal flashing.

Before we go up, tell us about any stain or damp patch you have seen inside. It shows us where water may already be getting in.

You get photos of what we found, so you can see the problem spots yourself. One homeowner who booked an inspection in December 2023 wrote in a Google review that the photos and a written report came the next day.

## Which Kinds of Roof Inspections Can You Book?

You can book a roof inspection after a storm, before you buy or sell a home, or as a yearly check on a roof that seems fine. Every one is for a house; Apex works on homes only.

Once a hurricane, tropical storm or strong thunderstorm has passed, a check shows whether the wind did harm you cannot see from the ground. Damage that is caught early is usually a smaller fix than a leak that shows up months later.

### Pre-Purchase and Home-Sale Roof Inspection

[NEED: client confirms this inspection type]

A roof is one of the costliest parts of a house to redo. Buyers want to know what they are taking on before closing. Sellers want to know what a buyer's inspector is likely to find, so there are no surprises late in the deal.

### Annual Maintenance Roof Inspection

[NEED: client confirms this inspection type]

A yearly look at a roof with no known problems lets small things get fixed while they are still small. A loose pipe boot or a few lifted shingles cost far less to deal with now than the water damage they can cause later.

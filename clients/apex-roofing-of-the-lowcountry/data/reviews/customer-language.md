### Themes (discovery-audit agent, 2026-10-07 · run 20261007-154441-discovery-64db)

Read from all 66 review texts. Quoted, never attributed (CM-027). Counts are approximate (themes overlap). These are customer impressions, not verified facts; they are planning input for headings, FAQ wording and Brand Guide Section I only.

1. **Communication and responsiveness** (most common, roughly 30 reviews) — fast call-backs, daily updates, questions answered.
   - "Communication was clear, fast and honest."
   - "They kept me updated on the progress"
2. **Explains the process; patient with questions** (roughly 15) — customers new to roofing, or nervous, felt informed.
   - "explained every detail of the process"
   - "always willing to explain anything in great detail"
3. **Honest assessment, no upselling** (roughly 10) — includes being told no work was needed.
   - "without trying to up sell me on additional services I did not need"
   - "find nothing that needed to be done"
4. **Clean job site** (roughly 15)
   - "Not a trace of rubbish was left behind."
   - "the place was left spotless"
5. **Leaks found and fixed quickly, sometimes after other roofers failed** (roughly 10)
   - "They found it immediately"
   - "It poured that Sunday all day and no leak."
6. **Photos and written reports** (roughly 8) — progress pictures, before/after photos, a report after inspection.
   - "Before and after photos are really useful."
   - "provided photos and a written report the next day"
7. **Fair, competitive price — not described as cheapest** (roughly 10)
   - "the price was fair"
   - "not the lowest it was in the expected price range"
8. **Local family business** (roughly 6) — father-and-son ownership is mentioned repeatedly.
   - "Local Family Biz"

**The one critical review (2 stars):** a hail-damage inspection — "did not take one photograph of the hail damages" and little support "throughout the claims process". It is the opposite of theme 6. The rating rounds to 5.0 (66 five-star, 1 two-star).

## Bluffton's Older Residents, Old Town and Wet Summers

Bluffton's older population has grown fast. At the 2020 census, 19.0% of Bluffton residents were 65 or older, up from 7.3% in 2010. The whole town is growing too, from 27,716 people in 2020 to an estimated 36,807 in 2025. Sun City Hilton Head, a planned community, sits nearby.

Old Town is the Bluffton Historic District, listed on the National Register. It sits on a bluff above the May River, and some of its homes date from the 1800s. The Church of the Cross held its first services in 1857, and the Heyward House Historical Center is also in Bluffton. The town has a humid subtropical climate, with about 50.4 inches of rain a year. Hurricanes are a threat in summer and early fall.

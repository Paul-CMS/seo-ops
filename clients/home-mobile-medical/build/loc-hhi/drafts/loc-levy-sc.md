## Levy's Rural Setting in Southern Jasper County

Levy is an unincorporated community in the southern part of Jasper County. The land there sits low, at 13 feet above sea level. South Carolina Highway 315 is the road that reaches Levy.

The county around Levy is rural, with many older residents. In the 2020 census, Jasper County counted 89.1% of its people as rural and 22.6% as 65 or older. Those are figures for the whole county, not for Levy alone.

To get a quote for a new lift at your Levy home, book a free in-home estimate. Home Mobile Medical comes out to see the stairs or the entrance where the lift would go, then quotes the lift that fits. For a stair lift, a photo of the stairs also works, priced by stair count.

### Who issues permits for a lift at a Levy home?

Jasper County Planning and Building issues permits for Levy homes. Levy is unincorporated, so it has no town permit office of its own. The county office is at 358 Third Ave in Ridgeland, and its phone number is 843-717-3650. Ask that office what a lift project at your address needs.

No, there is no trip fee to repair a lift at a Levy home, and no service-call fee for the visit either. That goes for any brand of stair lift or other lift. After you call, a visit is set up and the problem is diagnosed. You get a quote, any parts are ordered, and then the lift is fixed.

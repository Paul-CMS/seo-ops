## Yemassee's Rail-Line Setting Between Two Counties

Yemassee is a small town split between two counties. The CSX railroad line runs through it and divides Hampton County from Beaufort County. Most residents live on the Hampton County side. The town also has its own stop on the national rail network, the Yemassee Amtrak station. Savannah is about 44 miles to the south, and Charleston is about 54 miles to the east.

Summers here are hot and wet. The mean July high is 90.1 F, and the town gets about 53.6 inches of rain a year. Hampton County, home to most of Yemassee, is fully rural, and 19.8% of the county's residents were 65 or older in 2020. Hampton Regional Medical Center is in nearby Varnville, also in Hampton County. Just outside town stands Auldbrass Plantation, a house designed by Frank Lloyd Wright.

## One Metro Area Across Two Counties

The Hilton Head Island–Bluffton–Port Royal metropolitan area is made up of two counties, Beaufort and Jasper, home to 215,908 people at the 2020 census. More than one in four Beaufort County residents, 28.4%, were 65 or older that year. The metro's places are a mix of towns, unincorporated communities and census-designated places, and some sit across a county line. Okatie stretches from western Beaufort County into Jasper County, while Hardeeville is mostly in Jasper County with a small part in Beaufort County. Beaufort County also notes that FEMA flood insurance rate maps cover its coastal areas.

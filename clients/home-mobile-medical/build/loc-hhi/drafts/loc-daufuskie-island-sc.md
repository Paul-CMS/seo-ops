## Daufuskie Island's Ferry-Only Setting and Historic Landmarks

Daufuskie Island is reached only by ferry or barge. No bridge connects it to the mainland. The 2020 census counted 557 residents, and just over 400 of them live there full-time. People get around mostly by golf cart and bicycle, with some regular vehicles. In March 2026, Beaufort County began repairs at Melrose Landing.

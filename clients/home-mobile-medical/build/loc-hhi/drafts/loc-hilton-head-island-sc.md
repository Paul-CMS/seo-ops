## Hilton Head Island's Older Population and Tidal Setting

Hilton Head Island has an older population. At the 2020 census, 39.6% of residents were 65 or older. The median age was 59.3, in a population of 37,661. The island is home to Sea Pines Resort and to Harbour Town Golf Links.

The island has a humid subtropical climate, with about 51.4 inches of rain a year. Broad Creek, a tidal marsh, separates its two halves. The 1893 Sea Islands hurricane brought a 16-foot storm surge to the island. Mitchelville, the Freedom Park community for formerly enslaved people, is here too, and so is Hilton Head Island Airport.

### Who issues building permits for a lift on Hilton Head Island?

The Town of Hilton Head Island issues building permits through its Community Development Department. Its Building Service Center is at One Town Center Court, and the phone number is 843-341-4757. Applications go in through the town's Building & Development Citizen Self Service portal, or by email or delivery.

The town says a building permit is required to start a new construction or remodeling project. To find out what your lift project needs, call the Building Service Center. Its pages also cover flood hazard areas and flood elevation certificates.

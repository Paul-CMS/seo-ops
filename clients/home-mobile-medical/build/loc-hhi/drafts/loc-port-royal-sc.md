## Port Royal, the Sound and Its Military Neighbors

Port Royal sits on Port Royal Island, at the edge of Port Royal Sound. The town's historic center is the Old Village. The 1893 Sea Islands Hurricane destroyed much of the phosphate industry that once worked here. At the 2020 census, Port Royal had 4,764 housing units, and 13.8% of them stood vacant.

The town reaches well past the Old Village. Land at Marine Corps Recruit Depot Parris Island was annexed into Port Royal on October 11, 2000. U.S. Naval Hospital Beaufort is also one of the town's facilities.

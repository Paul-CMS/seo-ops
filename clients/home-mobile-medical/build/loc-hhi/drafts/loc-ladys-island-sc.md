## Lady's Island's Subdivisions and Sea Island Setting

Lady's Island is a mostly residential Sea Island across the Beaufort River from downtown Beaufort. Most of it is unincorporated Beaufort County, and small parts lie inside the City of Beaufort. The island sits about 16 feet above sea level. Much of its old plantation and timber land became housing subdivisions after the late 20th century. Coosaw Point, a historic site of the Coosaw people, is one of them today. The 2010 census counted 12,570 people on the island.

Two bridges link Lady's Island to the mainland, one to downtown Beaufort and one to Port Royal. The Richard V. Woods Memorial Bridge is a swing bridge. It carries traffic over the Beaufort River between downtown Beaufort and the island. Beaufort Executive Airport is on Lady's Island, and so is Beaufort Academy, a private K-12 school.

## Laurel Bay's Military Housing Setting

Laurel Bay is a military housing complex in Beaufort County, and it is also a census-designated place. It mainly houses military families stationed at Marine Corps Air Station Beaufort, Marine Corps Recruit Depot Parris Island and U.S. Naval Hospital Beaufort. The air station is about 5 kilometers from the housing complex.

The housing area opened in the mid-1950s. It had 1,955 housing units in 2000, and the 2020 census counted 5,082 residents. Chester Field, a historic site inside the housing area, was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1970.

## Okatie, Sun City and the Okatee River

Okatie is an unincorporated suburban community west of Hilton Head Island. About a quarter of its residents are 65 or older. Sun City Hilton Head, an age-restricted planned community, lies in the Okatie area and has close to 10,000 permanent residents.

The community takes its name from the Okatee River, an estuary of Port Royal Sound. The river and the community are spelled differently: Okatee for the water, Okatie for the place. South Carolina Highway 170 is also known as Okatie Highway.

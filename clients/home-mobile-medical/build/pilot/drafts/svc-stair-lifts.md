# Stair Lift Installation in the Lowcountry

Home Mobile Medical is a family-owned stair lift installer, locally owned and operated, that sources new stair lifts and installs them in homes across Beaufort County and the other towns it serves. If the stairs have become the hard part of your house, you likely want a lift in soon and one person in charge of it. Jason Workens, the founder and owner, supervises every installation himself. Start with a free in-home estimate, or send a photo of your stairs for a quote priced by stair count. Call (843) 476-1329.

## How Does a Stair Lift Go From Quote to Working Lift?

A stair lift from Home Mobile Medical goes from a free quote to a new lift, installed under Jason Workens' eye, in three steps.

1. **The quote.** Jason gives a free in-home estimate. If you would rather not wait for a visit, a photo of your stairs is enough for a quote. The price is set by the number of stairs.
2. **A new lift, sourced for you.** Home Mobile Medical does not make stair lifts and does not rent them. Jason orders a new lift for your staircase.
3. **The installation.** A stair lift on a straight staircase is usually installed in one day, in about 3–4 hours, and the same day in most cases. Jason and his helper do the work, and Jason personally supervises every installation.

Safety comes first. Jason holds every installation to the highest industry standards and the manufacturer's installation instructions.

Each stair lift carries the manufacturer's warranty. Home Mobile Medical does not add a warranty of its own. Financing is available, and Home Mobile Medical accepts common payment methods.

## What Types of Stair Lifts and Stair Lift Services Are Available?

Home Mobile Medical installs straight and curved stair lifts, and it maintains and repairs stair lifts of any brand.

### Straight Stair Lifts

A straight stair lift is built for a single flight of stairs with no turns. Home Mobile Medical sources each one new and installs it in your home.

### Curved Stair Lifts

A curved stair lift is built for stairs that turn or stop at a landing on the way up. Home Mobile Medical sources and installs curved stair lifts as well, and the curved stair lift page covers them in more detail.

### Stair Lift Maintenance and Repair

Home Mobile Medical maintains and repairs any stair lift, of any brand, within its service area. It does not matter who installed the lift.

### Stair Lift Brands

We install and repair stair lifts from leading brands such as Stannah, Bruno and Harmar. Home Mobile Medical is an independent installer and not a manufacturer.

Porch lifts and wheelchair lifts are separate services with their own pages. Home Mobile Medical does not rent stair lifts, and it does not sell lift chairs, scooters or ramps.

## What Do People Ask About Stair Lifts?

Most stair lift questions are about price, brands, install time, photo quotes and the shape of the stairs. Here are short answers based on how Home Mobile Medical works.

### How Much Does a Stair Lift Cost?

A stair lift's price depends mostly on your stairs: how many there are, and whether they need a straight or a curved lift. The number of stairs matters most, because quotes are priced by stair count. Home Mobile Medical's in-home estimate is free. A photo of the stairs also gets you a quote, and financing is available.

### Who Makes Stair Lifts?

Stair lifts are made by several companies, and leading brands include Stannah, Bruno and Harmar. Home Mobile Medical installs and repairs lifts from these brands. It is an independent installer, not a manufacturer, so it is not tied to any one brand when it sources a new lift for a home.

### How Long Does It Take to Install a Stair Lift?

Installing a stair lift on a straight staircase usually takes one day, about 3–4 hours, and is done the same day in most cases. At Home Mobile Medical, Jason Workens and his helper do the work, and Jason supervises the whole job.
### Can I Get a Stair Lift Quote From a Photo?

Yes, a clear photo of your stairs is enough for a stair lift quote, because the price is set by the number of stairs. That means you can get a number before anyone visits. If you would rather have someone look in person, the in-home estimate is free.

### What Is the Difference Between Straight and Curved Stair Lifts?

A straight stair lift runs up a staircase that climbs in one line, while a curved stair lift follows a staircase that turns or has a landing partway up. Home Mobile Medical installs both kinds, and a photo of the stairs or the free in-home estimate shows which one a home needs.

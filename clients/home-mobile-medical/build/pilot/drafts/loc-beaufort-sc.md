## Beaufort's Older Homes and Marshland Setting

The Beaufort Historic District covers 304 acres of the city and is a National Historic Landmark. Nearly 70 sites in the Beaufort area are listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Beaufort also has an older population. Census figures show 20.8% of city residents were 65 or older, and across Beaufort County the share was 28.4% in 2020. Two hospitals stand in the city: Beaufort Memorial Hospital, a 197-bed nonprofit acute-care hospital, and U.S. Naval Hospital Beaufort.

Beaufort sits amid a marshy estuary. Its climate is humid subtropical, with an average of 45.02 inches of precipitation a year. US 21 is Beaufort's major hurricane evacuation route, and the hurricane of 1893 caused extensive destruction in the city. Beaufort is also home to Marine Corps Air Station Beaufort.

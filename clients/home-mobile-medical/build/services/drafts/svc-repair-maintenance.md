## What Happens From the First Call to a Finished Fix?

A stair lift or lift repair with Home Mobile Medical follows six steps, from your first call to the finished fix.

1. **Your first call.** You tell us what is wrong with the stair lift or lift.
2. **A visit.** We set up a time to come to your home.
3. **A diagnosis.** We find the problem at that visit.
4. **A quote.** You get a quote for the repair.
5. **Parts, if needed.** We order the parts the repair calls for.
6. **The repair.** We complete the fix.

Home Mobile Medical has no service-call or trip charge. The steps are the same for any brand of stair lift or lift in the service area.

Repairs are handled by a team of two. That team is Jason Workens, the founder and owner, and his helper.

## Which Lifts and Repair Needs Does Home Mobile Medical Handle?

Home Mobile Medical repairs stair lifts, porch lifts and wheelchair lifts of any brand in its service area. It also offers ongoing maintenance and support.

### Stair Lift Repairs

Stair lift repairs cover both straight and curved stair lifts. Each one goes through the same six steps. The problem is found at the visit, so you do not need to know what broke before you call.

### Porch Lift and Wheelchair Lift Repairs

Porch lift and wheelchair lift repairs follow the same steps as a stair lift repair. A porch lift is also called a vertical platform lift. Home Mobile Medical's repair work is not limited to stair lifts. It covers any lift in the service area.

### Ongoing Maintenance and Support

Home Mobile Medical offers ongoing maintenance and support for stair lifts and other lifts. This is for lifts that are still working, not only for ones that have broken down.

### Lifts We Did Not Install

Home Mobile Medical repairs lifts it did not install, whatever the brand. It is an independent company, and it does not make lifts. The warranty on a lift comes from its manufacturer. Home Mobile Medical does not give a warranty of its own.

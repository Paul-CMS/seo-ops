## What Kinds of Porch Lift Jobs Does Home Mobile Medical Take On?

Home Mobile Medical takes on porch lift jobs at two kinds of raised spots, entrances and decks, and it keeps servicing lifts once they are in.

### Porch Lifts for Raised Entrances

A porch lift for a raised entrance is a vertical platform lift set beside a door that sits above the ground. It gives outdoor access from the yard or driveway up to the entry level. Home Mobile Medical sources the lift new and installs it at your entrance. Wheelchair lifts are a separate Home Mobile Medical service with their own page.

A porch lift for a deck is the same kind of vertical platform lift, placed so it reaches a raised deck instead of a front door. It links the ground to the deck level outside the home. The deck is looked at during the free in-home estimate, and the lift is then sourced new for it.

## What Do People Ask About Porch Lifts?

### What Is a Porch Lift?

A porch lift, also called a vertical platform lift, is a lift outside a home that carries a person up to a raised entrance or deck. It moves straight up from the ground to the level of the door or deck, so the steps are not needed.

## What Happens Between the Estimate and a Working Wheelchair Lift?

Three things happen: Home Mobile Medical visits for a free in-home estimate, buys a new wheelchair lift for your home, and installs it with Jason Workens in charge.

The visit costs nothing. There is no trip charge. It is when the place for the lift in your home is seen and the price is set.

The lift is bought new for you. Home Mobile Medical does not make wheelchair lifts, and it does not rent them.

Then the lift goes in. Stair lift installations typically take a couple of hours, and we can often complete two per day for curved, porch and wheelchair lifts. The actual timeline depends on project scope and is confirmed during your free in-home estimate. Jason supervises every installation, and he and his helper do the work.

Safety comes first. Jason holds every installation to the highest industry standards and the manufacturer's installation instructions.

Each wheelchair lift carries its manufacturer's warranty. Home Mobile Medical has no warranty of its own to add. Ask about financing at the estimate; it is available for wheelchair lifts.

## Which Wheelchair Lift Services Does Home Mobile Medical Offer?

Home Mobile Medical sources and installs new wheelchair lifts, and it maintains and repairs them after they are in.

### Wheelchair Lift Sourcing and Installation

A wheelchair lift carries a person, seated in a wheelchair, from one level of a home to another. Home Mobile Medical picks the lift after the free in-home estimate, not before. It then buys that lift new for your home and installs it there.

### Keeping a Wheelchair Lift Running

Home Mobile Medical maintains and repairs wheelchair lifts within its service area. That includes a wheelchair lift it did not put in, whatever the brand. Home Mobile Medical's repair page covers each step, from your first call to the finished fix.

### Wheelchair Lifts and Porch Lifts

Wheelchair lifts and porch lifts are two separate Home Mobile Medical services. The porch lift, also called a vertical platform lift, is the outdoor one. It is set at a raised entrance or a deck. Your free in-home estimate is where the choice between the two is made for your home.

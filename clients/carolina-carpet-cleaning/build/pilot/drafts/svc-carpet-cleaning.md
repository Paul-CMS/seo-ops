## How Do Professional Carpet Cleaners Clean a Carpet?

Professional carpet cleaners work in a set order: remove the dry soil, loosen the rest, pull the loosened soil out, groom the pile and dry the carpet. The [Carpet and Rug Institute (CRI)](https://carpet-rug.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Carpet-Preconditioning-Its-Impact-on-Cleaning.pdf) lists these steps from the IICRC S100 cleaning standard.

The work starts with dry soil removal. Dry grit that is not vacuumed up gets heavier and harder to remove once the carpet is wet.

Next comes preconditioning. A detergent solution goes onto the soiled carpet so the soil lets go of the fibers. It is put down with a sprayer, an in-line unit on the extraction machine or brush agitators. Then it is worked into the pile so it spreads evenly.

In hot water extraction, the method the CRI paper describes, hot water under pressure is sprayed into the pile and vacuumed right back out. The loose soil and extra water come out with it. Last, the pile is groomed and the carpet is dried as fast as possible. Fast drying also stops the musty smell a damp carpet can get.

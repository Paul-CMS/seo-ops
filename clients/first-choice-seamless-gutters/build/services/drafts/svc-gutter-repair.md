## Which Gutter Problems Can Be Repaired Instead of Replaced?

Most gutter problems that sit in one spot can be repaired. That covers a leaking corner or seam, a sagging run with loose hangers, a gutter that lost its slope, and an outlet that keeps backing up.

### Leaking Gutter Seams, Corners and Outlets

A leaking gutter lets water out where it should not, most often at a corner, a seam, an end cap or the outlet into the downspout. Over time that drip stains the wall, wets the fascia board behind the gutter and soaks the ground by the foundation. The inspection finds where the water escapes before anything is sealed or replaced.

### Sagging Gutters and Loose Hangers

A sagging gutter has pulled down or away from the roof edge, usually because the hangers that hold it have loosened. A low spot holds standing water, and that extra weight pulls the gutter down further. Reinforcing the hangers brings the run back up against the roof line.

### Gutter Pitch Correction

Gutter pitch is the slight slope that moves water toward the downspout. When a gutter loses that slope, water sits in the run or spills over the front edge instead of draining. Correcting the pitch lets the gutter empty the way it was built to.

### Overflow From Restricted Outlets and Downspouts

Overflow in a hard rain often means the water cannot leave fast enough. A restricted outlet, an undersized gutter or a downspout that drains in the wrong place can all cause it. Clearing the outlet or rerouting the water fixes many cases. A gutter that is simply too small for the roof is a capacity problem, and that one may call for a larger gutter.

### What is the most common problem with gutters?

Three common gutter problems are overflow, leaks and sagging, and one often leads to the next. A blocked outlet makes water sit in the gutter. The weight loosens the hangers, and the sagging run starts to drip at its corners and seams. A gutter that overflows in one hard rain is often too small, sloped wrong or draining to the wrong spot.

### Can a handyman fix a gutter?

A handyman can often handle a small gutter fix, like one loose hanger or a drip on a low, one-story gutter. A gutter contractor is the better call when a whole run sags, the slope is wrong, or water spills over in hard rain. The same goes for a gutter on a two-story roof. Those jobs need the slope and drainage checked.

### Should a damaged gutter be repaired or replaced?

Repair a damaged gutter when the problem is in one place, such as a loose hanger, a leaking corner or a blocked outlet.

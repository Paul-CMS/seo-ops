## Which Kinds of Aluminum Gutters Can a Home Have?

Aluminum gutters differ by shape, size, thickness and color, and each choice changes how much water the gutter holds and how it looks.

[NEED: which aluminum gutter profiles, sizes, gauges and colors First Choice Seamless Gutters installs — client, page:service_scope]

### K-Style Aluminum Gutters

K-style aluminum gutters have a flat back and a front edge shaped like crown molding. The flat back sits tight against the fascia board. The shape holds more water than a round gutter of the same width.

### Half-Round Aluminum Gutters

Half-round aluminum gutters are shaped like a pipe cut in half. They suit older or more traditional homes. They hang from brackets instead of sitting flat against the fascia.

### 5-Inch and 6-Inch Aluminum Gutters

Aluminum gutters come in widths, most often 5 inches and 6 inches across the top. A wider gutter carries more water before it spills. Large or steep roofs send more water to each run, so they may need the bigger size.

### Aluminum Downspouts

Aluminum downspouts carry water from the gutter to the ground. They come in sizes that match the gutter. A bigger downspout drains a full gutter faster in a heavy rain.

### Aluminum Gutter Colors

Aluminum gutters come with a baked-on painted finish in many colors. You can match the gutter to your trim, your fascia or your roof. The paint also keeps the bare metal from showing.

## What Should Polk County, Florida Homeowners Ask About Aluminum Gutter Installation?

### Are aluminum gutters good?

Yes, aluminum gutters are a good choice for most homes, because they are light, they do not rust, they come in many colors, and they cost less than copper. Their weak spot is dents: a ladder or a falling branch can bend a thin gutter. A thicker gauge holds up better.

### What is the difference between .027 and .032 aluminum gutters?

The difference is thickness: .027 aluminum gutters are 0.027 inches thick, and .032 gutters are 0.032 inches thick, so the .032 metal is stiffer, dents less and is less likely to sag under a load of wet leaves. The thicker metal also costs more. Ask which gauge any quote is based on.

### Do aluminum gutters rust?

No, aluminum gutters do not rust, because rust forms only on metals that contain iron, and aluminum has none. Aluminum can still wear in other ways. Its paint can fade over time. It can also corrode where it touches copper or bare steel, so the parts that hold it up matter.

### What do aluminum gutters cost for a home?

The cost of aluminum gutters for a home depends on how many feet of gutter the house needs, how high the roof is, the gauge and size of the gutter, how many downspouts it takes, and whether old gutters must come off first. First Choice Seamless Gutters prices each home after a free on-site visit.

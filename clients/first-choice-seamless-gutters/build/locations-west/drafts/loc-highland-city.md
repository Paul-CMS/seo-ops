## Where Is Highland City, and What Weather Do Its Roofs Face?

Highland City is an unincorporated community in Polk County, about 121 feet above sea level, with Lakeland Highlands as its neighbor. The Fort Fraser Trail, a 7.75-mile paved path that opened on December 9, 2006, runs through it, with a Highland City trailhead on 4th Street SE. The trail's north end lies near Lake Hancock and the Circle B Bar Reserve, north of Bartow. Fort Fraser, a U.S. Army fort set up in 1837, is part of the area's past. Neighborhoods include Fox Tree, off Crews Lake Drive, and Fallin Crest, and the area has many mobile homes.

Roofs in Highland City sit through the West Central Florida rainy season. The National Weather Service dates it from May 25 to October 10, with the peak from July to early September. That season brings heavy downpours and frequent lightning, and in its opening weeks, late May into June, hail and damaging winds join them. For about four and a half months a year, a Highland City gutter has to carry that water off the roof.

### How do Highland City's storms clog gutters?

Highland City's summer storms clog gutters by stripping leaves and twigs from trees over the roof and dropping them in all at once. Hail and strong wind are common from late May through June, and heavy rain keeps coming into October. A gutter that is already half full then spills over or backs up at the downspout.

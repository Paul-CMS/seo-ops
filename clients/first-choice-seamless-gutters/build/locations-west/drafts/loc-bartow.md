## What Should You Know About Bartow's Old Homes, Flat Land and Rain?

Bartow is the county seat of Polk County, and much of the town is old. Its landmarks include the 1909 Old Polk County Courthouse and the Bartow Downtown Commercial District. Houses in the Northeast Bartow Residential District were built from 1886 onward. The town has three historic districts on the National Register, all listed in 1993, and about 7,682 housing units in the 2020 Census.

Bartow's rain comes hard in summer. The town gets about 50.88 inches a year, most of it from June to September, and it sits in Florida's "lightning alley." Hurricane-force winds reach the Polk area less than once every ten years on average. The land is mostly flat, with a few gently rolling hills and sandy soil, and Lake Hancock lies just north of town. Rain that runs off a Bartow roof without a gutter lands on this level, sandy ground, where it has little fall to drain away from the walls.

## Bartow Gutter Questions Homeowners Ask

### Which gutter profile suits Bartow's historic homes?

On a historic Bartow home, First Choice Seamless Gutters fits half-round or custom profiles instead of the standard K-style shape. Built-in box gutters, also called Yankee gutters, can be relined and rebuilt instead of torn off. If your home sits in one of Bartow's three historic districts, ask the City of Bartow before you change the gutter style.

### Does gutter work at a Bartow address need a City of Bartow permit, and who issues it?

Inside Bartow city limits, building permits come from the City of Bartow Building Department, at City Hall, 450 N Wilson Avenue. A Bartow address outside city limits goes to the Polk County Building Division instead. To find out whether your gutter job needs a permit, call the city at (863) 534-0100, or the county at (863) 534-6080.

### What damage do Bartow's heavy summer rains do to undersized gutters?

In a hard Bartow downpour, an undersized gutter overflows, and water pours over its front edge onto walls, windows and the soil by the foundation. Bartow's heaviest rain falls from June to September, so a gutter that is too small for the roof shows it in those months first.

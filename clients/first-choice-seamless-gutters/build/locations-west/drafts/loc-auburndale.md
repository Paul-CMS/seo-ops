### What wears out gutters and fascia on older Auburndale homes?

Water does most of the damage. When a clogged or sagging gutter overflows in a summer storm, rain soaks the fascia board behind it. Over time, the wood softens and the gutter's fasteners loosen. On an older Auburndale home, years of those overflows add up.

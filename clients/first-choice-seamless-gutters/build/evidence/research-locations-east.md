### loc-davenport
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davenport,_Florida): incorporated 1915, Polk County, elevation 135 ft, "flatland interspersed with gently rolling hills," humid subtropical; population 2,888 (2010) to 9,043 (2020); 3,739 housing units in 2020 (mostly recent, fast-growth stock). No official source for drainage or failure patterns found.
- neighborhoods: ChampionsGate (11 subdivisions, master-planned; https://www.homesbymarco.com/neighborhoods/champions-gate-in-davenport-fl), Providence (gated golf), Solterra Resort, Highlands Reserve, Legacy Park, Posner Park (Wikipedia). Real-estate sources only; whether ChampionsGate and Solterra lie inside city limits was not checked.
- place_entities: Posner Park; Davenport City Hall (1 Allapaha St.); Horse Creek; AdventHealth Heart of Florida (only hospital in the city, per Wikipedia). No Wikidata IDs checked.
- permit_office: City of Davenport, 1 Allapaha St., (863) 419-3300, https://www.mydavenport.org/ (Polk PA permit agencies list). Official permit page not fetched; third-party sites say Building and Planning or Building Services.

### loc-haines-city
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haines_City,_Florida): Lake Wales Ridge, "a sandy terrain consisting of a low ridge of gently rolling hills," porous sandy soil, elevation 157 ft; 11,006 housing units (2020), fast growth at city edges (Orlando and Disney access). No official drainage source.
- neighborhoods: Oakland (historic district), Randa Ridge, Southern Dunes (golf, developed 1995-2005), Posner Park (Wikipedia); Hamilton Bluff (Lennar listing, new-home community).
- place_entities: Lake Eva Park (2009, with Aquatics Center); Polk Hotel (now Landmark Baptist College); historic Oakland district; Haines City Public Library (111 N. 6th St.) (Wikipedia).
- permit_office: Building Division, Development Services Dept., 620 East Main Street, Haines City FL 33844, 863-421-3600; contractors must register first. https://www.hainescity.com/159/Contractor-Requirements (read 2026-10-27).

### loc-lake-alfred
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Alfred,_Florida): city incorporated 1915, elevation 131 ft, Central Florida Highlands "flatland interspersed with gently rolling hills"; citrus-research town (UF/IFAS Citrus Research and Education Center, 1917). No housing-age source.
- neighborhoods: Downtown Lake Alfred, Lucerne Park, Sweetwater Golf and Tennis Club, Water Ridge, Golf Grounds Estates-Aqua Vista, Lake Mariana Shores (Redfin and listing pages; not official).
- place_entities: Mackay Gardens and Lakeside Preserve; UF/IFAS Citrus Research and Education Center; Lake Alfred Public Library (245 N. Seminole Ave.); Lake Alfred Historical Society (Wikipedia).
- permit_office: City of Lake Alfred Building Inspection Division / Community Development, 120 E Pomelo St., (863) 291-5748, permits@mylakealfred.com; Accela portal https://aca-prod.accela.com/COLA ; page https://mylakealfred.com/262/Building-Permit (search summary; page text not fetched).

### loc-lake-hamilton
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Hamilton,_Florida): town of 1,537 (2020), elevation 131 ft, centred on 2,184-acre Lake Hamilton (average depth 7.2 ft), part of the Chain of Lakes; incorporated 1924; SR 17 Scenic Highway. A news report (Yahoo, date not shown) says 261 building permits in a year vs 1 in 2022, so growth is rapid.
- neighborhoods: Hamilton Bluff (new-home community, Lennar/Meritage listings). Only one found; fewer than 3.
- place_entities: Lake Hamilton (lake); Bonars Island; Town Hall (Wikipedia); Ridge Scenic Highway (SR 17; https://floridascenichighways.com/our-byways/central-region/ridge-scenic-highway/). Partial.
- permit_office: Town of Lake Hamilton Permitting Department, https://www.townoflakehamilton.com/building ; PO Box 126, (863) 439-1910 (Polk PA list).

### loc-dundee
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dundee,_Florida): town, settled 1911, incorporated 1925, elevation 213 ft (highest of the nine), Humid subtropical; April 2024 referendum kept town status; SR 17 runs through the centre.
- neighborhoods: Ridge of Dundee, The Ridge at Swan Lake, Lake Ruth Estates, Mabel Loop Ridge (listing sites; county-record names also include Bluffs Dundee, Crystal Lake Preserve, Dell Lake Village). Not official.
- place_entities: Dundee Town Hall and Library; Lake Marie Park (listing-site mention only); Ridge Scenic Highway (SR 17). Partial.
- permit_office: Town of Dundee Development Services Department, 124 Dundee Road, (863) 438-8330 ext. 223 or 438-8335 (town pages differ); https://townofdundee.com/departments/building-services/ ; CO requests co-info@townofdundee.com.

### loc-lake-wales
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Wales,_Florida): on the Lake Wales Ridge, elevation 128 ft, population 16,361 (2020); founded 1911-12, city 1925; hurricane-force winds from Charley, Frances and Jeanne in 2004 and from Irma in 2017; 1920s land-boom commercial historic district.
- neighborhoods: Lake Ashton (gated active community), Mountain Lake, Carlsberg Estates, Chalet Estates, Lake Haven Estates, Mohawk Groves (listing sites; Wikipedia names none).
- place_entities: Bok Tower Gardens; Spook Hill; Lake Wales Commercial Historic District; Lake Wales History Museum; Chalet Suzanne; Grassy Lake Preserve (Wikipedia).
- permit_office: City of Lake Wales Building Division, Municipal Administration Building, 201 W. Central Ave., Permitting@LakeWalesFL.gov, 8-5 Mon-Fri; https://lakewalesfl.gov/241/Building-Division (search summary). The city application form cites the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (an older form; confirm current edition).

### loc-frostproof
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frostproof,_Florida): Lake Wales Ridge, elevation 82 ft, between Lake Clinch and Lake Reedy; name tied to the 1894-95 freeze that destroyed citrus; city since 1960. A Clinchwood profile flags flood and drainage exposure near Lake Clinch (real-estate page, not official).
- neighborhoods: Clinchwood, North Reedy Shore, Lake Reedy Hills, Lake Reedy Cove, Lake Side Terrace, Oakridge Estates (listing sites; not official).
- place_entities: Historic Ramon Theater (1925); historic Atlantic Coast Line depot (1912); Lake Clinch; Lake Reedy; Frostproof Middle-Senior High School (Wikipedia).
- permit_office: City of Frostproof Building Department, https://cityoffrostproof.com/departments/building/ ; main (863) 635-7850; building line 635-7854 or 635-7855 (sources disagree); contractors must register.

### loc-fort-meade
- local_context: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Meade,_Florida): oldest city in Polk County (incorporated 1885), elevation 144 ft, flat with gently rolling hills; 5,100 people (2020); Fort Meade Historic District. TopHap street data: median year built 1974 (aggregator, not official).
- neighborhoods: Downtown Fort Meade, Stoneybrook Golf and Country Club (Redfin listings; not official). Fewer than 3.
- place_entities: Patterson Park; Fort Meade Outdoor Recreation Area; Old Fort Meade School House Museum; Christ Church (Episcopal, 1889; used in the 1990 HBO film Judgment); Fort Meade Public Library (75 East Broadway) (Wikipedia).
- permit_office: City of Fort Meade, PO Box 856, (863) 285-1119 (Polk PA list); https://www.cityoffortmeade.org/ . Which department issues permits is unconfirmed (shed-vendor pages say city hall); no official page found.

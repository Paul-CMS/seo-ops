## What Makes Lakeland Hard on Gutters?

Lakeland's heavy summer rain is what tests a gutter most. The city gets about 56.8 inches of rain a year, and most of it falls from June to September. The National Weather Service puts the area's rainy season at about May 25 to October 10. Hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so the wettest weeks and the storm months overlap. In those months, a gutter that is clogged, sagging or too small spills water at the roof edge.

That rain falls on very different parts of Lakeland. Some homes face Lake Morton or sit near Lake Mirror Park. Others stand in the Dixieland Historic District, on the streets around Florida Southern College, or out in Lakeland Highlands. Wherever the house sits, its roof has to shed that summer rain without spilling it against the walls.

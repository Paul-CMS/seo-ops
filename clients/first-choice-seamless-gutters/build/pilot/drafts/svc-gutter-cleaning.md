## What Does a Gutter Cleaning Include, and What Is Separate?

A gutter cleaning from First Choice Seamless Gutters includes four parts: debris removal, outlet and elbow clearing, downspout flushing, and a check of where the water exits. Each part fixes a different kind of clog.

### Gutter Debris Removal

Gutter debris removal takes out the leaves, twigs and grit that sit along the gutter run. The crew works by hand or with a gutter scoop, and uses a blower only for loose, dry debris. Clearing the run lets water reach the outlets instead of pooling and spilling over the front edge.

### Outlet and Elbow Clearing

Outlet and elbow clearing opens the spots where the gutter drains into the downspout and where the downspout bends. These are the narrowest points in a rain gutter system. Fine debris packs in here first, so a gutter can look empty and still back up.

### Downspout Flushing and Water-Exit Check

Downspout flushing pushes water from a hose through each downspout until it runs free at the bottom. The crew then checks that the water leaves the downspout exit and moves away from the house. A downspout that drips or stays dry during the flush still has a blockage.

### Gutter Maintenance Checks on Every Cleaning

Gutter maintenance is part of each cleaning, not a separate service. While the gutters are open, the crew checks the elbows and the downspout exits, so you know the whole system drains. Anything that needs repair is noted as separate work.

### Work That Is Not Part of a Gutter Cleaning

Three jobs sit outside a gutter cleaning. Gutter repair is priced on its own. Underground drains are a separate job. Washing the outside face of the gutters is not part of a cleaning either. If your gutters fill up fast, gutter guards can cut down how often they need cleaning.

### When is the best time of year to clean gutters in Florida?

In Florida, the best time to clean gutters is shortly before the rainy season starts, about late May (the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/tbw/RainySeason) puts the rainy season at May 25 to October 10), and again after the trees over your roof drop their heaviest load of leaves. So your trees matter more than the calendar.

### How often should gutters be cleaned in Polk County?

How often depends on the trees over your roof; homes under trees need it more often, because leaves and fine grit build up in outlets and downspouts even when the gutters look clear from the ground. Gutter guards can stretch the time between cleanings.

### What are the signs my gutters need cleaning?

The clearest signs that gutters need cleaning are water spilling over the front edge in a storm, plants growing out of the gutter, gutters that sag or pull away from the roof, stains on the wall below, and downspouts that drip or stay dry while it rains. One sign is worth a look. A free check shows where the clog sits.

### Do telescopic gutter cleaners work?

Telescopic gutter cleaners can pull loose, dry leaves out of a low, one-story gutter from the ground, but they cannot show you what is packed in the outlets, clear a blocked elbow or flush a downspout, which is where many overflow problems start. They help with light upkeep. They do not replace a full clean.

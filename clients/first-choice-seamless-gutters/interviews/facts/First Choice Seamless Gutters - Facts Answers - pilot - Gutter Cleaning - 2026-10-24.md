# First Choice Seamless Gutters — facts answers (pilot batch, Gutter Cleaning), 2026-10-24

Answers relayed by Paul from the owner. Saved as given.

1. Is the site's list of cleaning steps what a cleaning visit covers?

   Yes, with a clarification about repairs. Standard cleaning includes removing leaves, pine needles and other debris, clearing gutter outlets, checking elbows for blockages, flushing downspouts, and making sure water exits freely at the accessible discharge points. If leaks, loose hangers, sagging gutters or other damage are found, they are pointed out and repairs are discussed. Repairs are not automatically included in the cleaning price. Clearing connected underground drains is separate.

2. How is a cleaning visit done, and what equipment is used?

   Start by checking the gutters, downspouts and access around the house. Work is generally done from ladders, removing the bulk of the debris by hand or with a gutter scoop, then the debris is collected and the work area cleaned up. Once the gutters are clear, a garden hose rinses them, flushes the downspouts, and checks that water drains properly. Outlets and elbows are checked for clogs, and the owner is told if a blockage needs disassembly or extra work. A blower may be used for loose, dry debris when appropriate. Pressure washing is not part of the normal interior gutter-cleaning process; washing the outside of the gutters is a separate service.

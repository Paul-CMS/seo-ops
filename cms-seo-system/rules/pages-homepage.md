# Homepage (purpose: brand authority + E-E-A-T trust + gateway to services and locations)

The order below is a strong guideline, not mandatory (ruled).

HP-01 Review bar above nav — no H tag; platform logos, stars, counts, links.
HP-02 Navigation — top-level pages only.
HP-03 H1 — "[The Best per H1-04] [Service variant] in [Area variant]". One H1.
HP-04 Opening paragraph — semantic triple, area variants, phone, free estimate, under 500 tokens.
HP-05 H2 "[GB Category] Services" — the ONLY H2 introducing services.
HP-06 H3 anchor links to each top-level service page (max 5–6, no descriptions).
HP-07 Trust/About — H2 with brand + area entity; sub-items are styled cards, not H3s.
HP-08 How It Works — 3 steps, styled label and bold step titles, no H tags.
HP-09 Social proof bar — no H tag. Review counts appear 3x: review bar, hero badge (aggregate), proof bar (per platform).
HP-10 Gallery — H2 with service + area variants. Alt text: "[Service] in [City], [Full State] — [Brand]", rotating cities.
HP-11 Testimonials — H2 with area + brand; pick reviews that mention places and services.
HP-12 FAQ — H2 + H3 per question; brand-level questions only (licensing, deposits, property types, emergencies, brands serviced).
HP-13 Service areas grid — H2 with area variant; cities not H-tagged; links to all location pages.
HP-14 Google Maps GBP embed — no H tag.
HP-15 Featured blog posts — H2 with service + area; only after blog redirects are live.
HP-16 Final CTA — H2 with area entity, phone, buttons.
HP-17 Footer — NAP identical to citations, quick links, social, no H tags.
HP-18 Schema reference implementation: woodlawntrees.com. On-page reference: this file.

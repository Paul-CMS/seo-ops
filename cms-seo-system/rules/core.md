# Core

CORE-01 Entity-based, AI-search-first. Google and AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews) resolve entities; never apply keyword-matching SEO thinking.
CORE-02 Inconsistency = suppression. Every statement about who/what/where is checked against verifiable data (Wikipedia, knowledge graph, citations).
CORE-03 Pillar 1, Branded Location Authority: citations, GBP, schema sameAs, ID page, press releases, GBP posts to citation URLs.
CORE-04 Pillar 2, Site Efficiency: silo architecture, one page per service, one page per city, complete internal link wheel, no service+city pages.
CORE-05 Diagnose every underperforming campaign through both pillars first.
CORE-06 Never claim unverified information. Review-backed practices are welcome. Generic information is allowed only as a temporary placeholder when the build cannot wait for client verification; it is flagged in the draft (SVC-16) and replaced once verified.
CORE-07 Research before asking. Use collected evidence (reviews, discovery) as references.
CORE-08 Paul may confirm company facts as owner-proxy. Anyone else running the work gets company facts approved by Paul.
CORE-09 Sensitive industries (chiropractic, medical, insurance, legal, financial): Paul approves, and the client's licensed practitioner signs off on health claims.

## Inputs

IN-01 Business identity: exact formal name (never abbreviated or varied), primary + secondary GBP categories, canonical URL, exact address format, one phone format, storefront vs SAB, founding year.
IN-02 Service area: every city served, grouped by county.
IN-03 Current assets: GBP URL(s), citation count + known citation URLs, press/media URLs, social URLs, ID page URL, review counts per platform.
IN-04 Website data: full crawl (URL, title, H1, word count). Novamira supplies the on-site inventory read-only.
IN-05 Competitors: top 3–5 Maps competitors, their ranked keywords and backlink anchors via DataforSEO; citation counts via BrightLocal. OpenSEO not used.
IN-06 GSC, GA4 and backlink data are optional evidence. Consolidation rests on the system's own research; no suggestion where research can't support one.
IN-07 Screaming Frog (free licence: no JS rendering, no custom extraction) is the crawl of record. Missing rendered-HTML data is never a finding. Novamira inventory is cross-checked against it for orphans, drafts and noindex.
IN-08 The intake confirmations (client folder `[Client Name] - TMS`, evidence uploaded, GSC/GA4/backlinks available?, subdomains/microsites?) are the FIRST message of every client conversation or audit.
IN-09 Missing inputs: if a missing answer can be filled with a generic placeholder, the build proceeds and is updated when the client replies (CORE-06). Otherwise block only on business identity/NAP and the crawl; batch every other question.

# Location pages (lean; each city unique)

LOC-01 H1 — "[The Best per H1-04] [Service variant] in [City]". No state.
LOC-02 Opening paragraph — who + what + this city, phone, free estimate; a different angle every city.
LOC-03 H2 "[Brand] services in [City]" — H3 links to ALL service pages, no descriptions.
LOC-04 H2 "[Service] in [City]" — 3–5 sentences on this city's real conditions as they affect the service (housing stock, terrain, weather, development age, failure patterns). Never reused.
LOC-05 FAQ — H3 per question, 3–4 city-only questions, never reused.
LOC-06 H2 Neighborhoods — 6–12 real neighborhoods as a list; fewer is fine.
LOC-07 City-level map embed. GBP map only on the homepage and the GBP's own city page.
LOC-08 No "Things to Do" or "Top Sites" sections. Real landmarks and POIs from local research are woven naturally into the copy to build entity associations.
LOC-09 H2 sibling cities — links to every other location page.
LOC-10 CTA — phone, free estimate, NAP.
LOC-11 Target under 1,000 words; may stretch to ~1,500 only for cause-and-effect local conditions tied to the services, unique to the city, every H2 under 500 tokens. Never service descriptions.
LOC-12 Pages unique and non-overlapping. Local research is niche-filtered, never generic town facts.
LOC-13 Routine location questions don't reach Paul; apply defaults.
LOC-14 Location Page Optimizer is planned. Until then existing location pages follow the build path.

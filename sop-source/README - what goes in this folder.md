## Do not put here

- `GLOBAL-Bradley-Entity-Methodology-Reference.md` — superseded by the Standard. Two versions of the methodology in one folder is the exact duplicate-brain problem this structure exists to prevent.
- Client-specific evidence or deliverables. Those live in the client folder.
- Agent or gem instructions. Those are in `Resources/`.

---

## Two things worth knowing

**Rule IDs are the interface.** The agents and gems cite IDs rather than restating rules. That way a rule lives in exactly one place and cannot drift between copies.

**Grades describe sourcing, not authority.** A grade-C rule is in force like any other. The grade tells you what to say when a client asks why, and which transcripts to chase first.

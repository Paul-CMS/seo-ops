## What this package is

Everything we hold on the Bradley Benner / Semantic Links methodology, consolidated into
one rule set, with Bradley's methodology separated from our own additions.

Before this, the methodology was spread across nine execution documents — the stage SOPs,
the BB System package, four video transcripts and two synthesis documents. It worked, but
nobody could point to where Bradley's rules ended and ours began. That distinction matters
operationally: it decides which rules can be overridden by evidence and who has to approve
the override.

## The main finding

The two documents our governing SOPs cite as "Bradley's core source documents" — the
*Local SEO Analysis System Prompt* and the *Website Build & SEO SOP* — **are not written
by Bradley.** Each says so in its own text:

> *"…as derived from full SEO audit videos, entity analysis videos, semantic triple
> transcripts, geographic hierarchy transcripts, AI SEO transcripts, location page
> transcripts, GBP posts transcripts, press release transcripts, subdomain strategy
> transcripts, and live implementation analysis…"*

> *"Generalized from full methodology"*

They are syntheses of his videos made by someone else. That places them one authority
level below where four of our stage SOPs currently treat them, and it means nine
underlying transcripts are named as sources and none are in our possession.

**This is not a reason to distrust the methodology.** The two documents are internally
consistent, they agree with each other, and they agree with the four transcripts we do
hold wherever the two overlap. It is a reason to know which rules we can quote him on and
which we can only attribute to the framework.

## How the rules are graded

| Grade | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 33 | Traceable to a transcript we hold. Quotable. |
| **B** | 44 | In both synthesis documents, mutually consistent, original source not held. |
| **C** | 5 | Single-source, unverifiable. |

Two that matter:

**The semantic triple (BB-301) is grade B.** The synthesis documents call it the most
important concept in the methodology. It appears in no transcript we hold.

**"The Best" in every H1 (BB-305) is grade C.** Described as a "confirmed AI search trigger
word" with no source cited anywhere. It opens the H1 of every service and location page in
the portfolio and has not been re-verified since the original GutterXperts work. A
single-variable cohort test is specified and pending.

A grade describes **how well-sourced a rule is, not how binding it is.** Every rule in the
Standard is in force. The grade tells us what to say when a client asks why, and what to
retrieve first.

## What the Delta covers

18 entries against 83 rules — most of the methodology is followed exactly as written.

The governing principle behind the largest entry: **the methodology governs how things are
built; evidence governs whether things are destroyed.** Construction follows the Standard
with no evidence required. Consolidation, redirects, deletions and spend produce a
*candidate* that evidence then decides. Bradley's architecture rules were derived from what
works when building — they say nothing about what an existing page currently earns.

That is what prevents a technically correct consolidation from removing a page that is
generating leads.

## What changes next

The stage SOPs should stop containing methodology and start citing rule IDs. That is the
work this package makes possible: one place where a rule lives, one place where our
departure from it is recorded, and execution documents that carry sequence, ownership and
gates instead of restating rules that drift out of sync the moment one copy is edited.
